How to move your Trak'n'Turn data forward

← Epic Custom Apps

Trak'n'Turn helps you track what's important to you. Its newest version adds a simple way to carry your tracking data over to Log It All — our flexible logging app — so your history comes with you.

Moving your data to Log It All

Log It All is a free app that records timestamped entries for anything you track. Trak'n'Turn can export your tracking data as a file, and Log It All can import it — your categories become Log It All categories, your items become events, and each logged occurrence becomes an entry.

  1. In Trak'n'Turn, open Trak'n'Turn Help and tap Export Data (for Log It All). Save or share the file it creates.
  2. Install Log It All from the App Store (it's free) if you don't have it yet.
  3. In Log It All, open the Tools tab and tap Import from Trak'n'Turn…, then choose the file you saved.

That's it — your tracking history is now in Log It All. Full details are on the Help tab inside Log It All.

What about “whose turn is it?”

Trak'n'Turn could also track whose turn it is. That turn-taking side isn't part of the Log It All export — Log It All is a logger, not a turn tracker.

Interested in moving your turn-taking groups? Our app Give A Chit is built for turn-taking. We haven't built an automatic path for it yet — if you'd like one, email us and we'll consider it. The more interest we hear, the sooner we'll look into it.
